How the estimate works

A hand-coded graph of California's freeways and state highways is routed from downtown with class speeds (rural freeway 68 mph, metro freeway 50, two-lane highway 52, mountain pass 38). Each cell adds the straight-line hop to the nearest road at 30 mph.

State and county lines are Census cartographic boundaries (us-atlas).
