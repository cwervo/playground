p2p-motion · motion-triggered mini camera → room hub simulator

Simulates the UW insect-scale wireless camera (Iyer et al., Science Robotics 2020): a milligram-scale camera board rides a beetle, an accelerometer wakes it only when the beetle moves, it captures a 160×120 monochrome frame, JPEG-compresses it, and radios the packets to a more powerful hub across the room, which stores and processes the photos. Network-last: everything runs and persists on this device (localStorage).
