[github.com/worrydream/EarlyHistoryOfSmalltalk](https://github.com/worrydream/EarlyHistoryOfSmalltalk),
Bret Victor's annotated, reader-repaired hypertext edition of Alan Kay's
"The Early History of Smalltalk" (HOPL II, 1993), also published at
<https://worrydream.com/EarlyHistoryOfSmalltalk/>.
